Our Activities

Memorable moments that made our senior year unforgettable.

Visit to Chiminike

Our class visited Chiminike as part of an educational experience. During the trip, we explored interactive exhibits, strengthened teamwork, and enjoyed learning through science and technology in a fun environment.

TES Projects

Throughout the school year, we worked on different TES projects that allowed us to develop research, creativity, communication, and teamwork skills while applying what we learned in class.

Visit to the ICF

Our visit to the ICF gave us the opportunity to learn more about Honduras' cultural heritage and the importance of protecting the country's historical and archaeological treasures.

Bake Sales

The Seniors'26 class organized several bake sales to raise funds for our graduation activities. These events promoted teamwork, responsibility, and participation from the entire school community.

Valentine's Day Celebration

We organized a special Valentine's Day celebration with decorations, music, games, and activities that brought students together and created a joyful atmosphere across the school.

ERA 2026 Elections

The Seniors'26 class was responsible for organizing and managing the ERA 2026 student elections. We coordinated logistics, supervised the voting process, and helped ensure a fair and successful event.
